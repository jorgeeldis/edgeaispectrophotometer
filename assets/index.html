Operating Guide

Quick Start

HOME Tab

1 - Baseline: Insert reference cuvette for baseline, distilled water is recommended

2 - Settings: Adjust settings if needed.

3 - Single Scan or Continuous: Insert sample cuvette for scanning, wait for the measurement to finish.

4 - Save Data: Save measurement to database, begin AI analysis and create reports

MEASURES Tab

1 - Review all measurements that are saved alongside with their name and category

2 - Click a measurement to send csv via email or hide from view (will remain in database)

ANALYSIS Tab

1 - Select measurements to check metrics against reference sample

2 - If measurement has no category / reference sample, will appear no metric

MAINTENANCE Tab

1 - Check device health and recommended actions

REPORTS Tab

1 - Review PDF reports saved with AI/ML analysis

2 - Click a report to send PDF via email or hide from view (will remain in database)

CHAT Tab

1 - Chat with a trained AI engine anything related to spectrophotometer, measurements, analysis and device health.
